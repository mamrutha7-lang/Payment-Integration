Leave policy

  • Signatures · one, the Main Admin’s: your Sanction or Reject is the decision, and you cannot decide your own request. Your name, the time and your uploaded signature image print in the PROGRAM DIRECTOR block.
  • Who sees a request · the Main Admin alone, staff leave included. Faculty apply, withdraw and read their own; nobody else opens the queue.
  • Allowances · recorded per person, per printed option, per academic year, behind Edit. An account with no allowance recorded is not measured against one, which is not a balance of zero.
  • Refusing · this console will not send a refusal without remarks, and the applicant reads them.
