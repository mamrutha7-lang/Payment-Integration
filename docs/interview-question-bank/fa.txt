# FA — Financial Analytics
# Track code: fa   Interviewer: a sharp, risk-conscious Managing Director / CFO
# (voice: matthew)
# Paste into Main Admin -> Interview questions -> Financial Analytics -> Add many.
# Frameworks the persona already carries: DCF modelling, financial ratios, risk
# mitigation, valuation techniques, M&A frameworks.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- opening --
[opening] Tell me about yourself and why finance rather than one of the other specialisations.
[opening] Walk me through your resume, and stop at the point where you first worked with a real set of financial statements.
[opening] Which area interests you most — valuation, risk, corporate finance or financial planning and analysis — and why?
[opening] Tell me about a company whose financials you have actually read. What stood out?
[opening] What is happening in the markets this week that a finance graduate should have an opinion on?

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- probing --
[probing] Walk me through how a ten rupee depreciation expense flows through the three financial statements.
[probing] A company is profitable and running out of cash. Give me three ways that happens.
[probing] What is the difference between the direct and indirect methods of building a cash flow statement?
[probing] Explain working capital, and tell me what a rising working capital requirement says about a growing business.
[probing] What is EBITDA, why do people use it, and what does it deliberately hide?
[probing] Explain the difference between enterprise value and equity value, and how you bridge one to the other.
[probing] Which three ratios would you look at first to judge whether a company can service its debt?
[probing] Explain return on equity, and then break it down using the DuPont identity.
[probing] What is the difference between operating leverage and financial leverage, and which is more dangerous in a downturn?
[probing] Explain the time value of money to someone who has never taken a finance class.
[probing] What is WACC, what goes into it, and why does it rise when a company takes on too much debt?
[probing] Explain beta, and tell me what it does and does not measure about a company's risk.
[probing] Why is the cost of debt usually lower than the cost of equity?
[probing] What is the difference between a bond's coupon and its yield, and what happens to the price when rates rise?
[probing] Explain accrual accounting versus cash accounting with an example where the two give opposite pictures.
[probing] What is deferred revenue, where does it sit, and why do investors watch it?
[probing] Explain the difference between a strategic buyer and a financial buyer, and why they pay different prices.
[probing] What is due diligence, and name three things you would look for that do not appear in the financial statements.
[probing] How would you build a simple three-statement model, and in what order?
[probing] What is sensitivity analysis, and what question is it actually answering for the decision maker?

# -------------------------------------------------------------- deep_dive --
[deep_dive] Walk me through a discounted cash flow valuation from the first line to the equity value per share.
[deep_dive] Your DCF says the company is worth four hundred crore and the market says nine hundred. Which is wrong, and how would you find out?
[deep_dive] How do you calculate terminal value, and why do the two standard methods often disagree by so much?
[deep_dive] A company wants to acquire a competitor at a thirty percent premium. Tell me how you would decide whether that is a good deal.
[deep_dive] Explain accretion and dilution in an all-stock acquisition, and what would make you advise against it even if it is accretive.
[deep_dive] A business unit earns a twelve percent return on capital against a cost of capital of fourteen. It is growing quickly. What do you recommend?
[deep_dive] How would you value a company with negative earnings and eighty percent revenue growth?
[deep_dive] The CFO wants to change a revenue recognition policy in a way that is technically permitted and materially flattering. What do you do?
[deep_dive] Walk me through the risks you would flag before signing off on a large foreign currency borrowing.
[deep_dive] A portfolio company breaches a debt covenant next quarter on current projections. Take me through the options, in the order you would attempt them.
[deep_dive] Build the case for and against a share buyback for a company sitting on a large cash balance and a falling share price.
[deep_dive] How would you stress test a business plan, and which assumption would you break first?
[deep_dive] Two divisions want the same capital and both have positive net present value. How do you choose, and what non-financial factor could override your maths?
[deep_dive] You find an inconsistency in a model that a deal team has already presented to the client. Walk me through exactly what you do.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- wrap_up --
[wrap_up] Which part of finance do you find hardest, and how are you working on it?
[wrap_up] What would you want to be genuinely good at by the end of your first year here?
[wrap_up] Tell me the one thing about your candidacy that you think I have underestimated.
[wrap_up] What questions do you have for us about the role, the team, or the deals you would work on?
