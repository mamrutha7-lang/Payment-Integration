REEPMain Admin / Governance

Governance

Two instruments, deliberately separate. Faculty access grants a capability to a person or a group — nobody has it until you say so. Student features are on for everyone by default and switched off down the hierarchy: a whole specialization, one batch, or a single student.

shield_lock

Why the two are not one setting

Faculty access is deny by default. A capability is checked after the mentor-group rule, so granting student.interviews reaches only students already in that mentor's group. Except the programme-wide ones — analytics, exports, the hierarchy — which have no group to narrow to and hand over the whole college.

Student features are allow by default. Switching the voice interviewer off for a specialization is not a permission, it is availability, and the most specific rule wins: a batch-level switch overrides its specialization, and a single student overrides their batch.
